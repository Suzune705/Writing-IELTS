*More and more universities are replacing face-to-face teaching with online courses.
Do you think this is a positive and negative development*

- In recent years, there has been a growing trend among universities to switch from traditional face-to-face teaching to online courses. This lead to a debate about whether this shift is a positive or negative development. In my opinion , while online courses offer certain advantages, such as flexibility and accessibility, they also have significant drawbacks that cannot be ignored. 


Body 1 :
 - thesis statement : I believe that the shift towards online courses has both positive and negative aspects and it is important to cnsider both sides of the argument before making a conclusion.
 - 
 - supporting idea 1 : On the positive side , online courses provide students with greater flexibility in terms of when and where they can access course materials. This can be particularly beneficial for students who don't have the opportunity to attend traditional classes due to work or family commitments. Additionally , online courses can also be very convenient for students who want to review lessons and prewatch lectures at their own pace. This can help students to prepare better and also to keep up with the course content more effectively.

Body 2 :
  - supporting idea 2 : However , although online courses offer flexibility and convenience , they also have substantial limitations. One of the main drawbacks is the lack of interaction between students and instructors. When students attend traditional classes, the tend to concentrate more and engage in discussions with their peers and teachers. In contrast, in online courses, students are susceptible to distractions and often do personal work while watching lectures. As a result, the may miss part of their knowledge about the course content and also lose the opportunity to develop critical thinking and communication skills through class discussions. Moreover , attending online courses can require a reliable internet access and appropriate devices which are not always available to students who live in rural areas. For example , some studens living in remote areas may not have access to high-speed internet, so they can struggle to participate in online courses and may fall behind their peers who have better access to technology.
