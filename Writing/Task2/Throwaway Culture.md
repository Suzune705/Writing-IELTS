Introduction: 
  - In many contries, people increasingly replace broken items instead of reparing them
This trend is mainly caused by the falling cost of new products and the inconvenience of repair , and it may lead to more waste and a decline in repair habits


Body 1 : 
      - One reason for this trend is that many new products are now relatively cheap. Mass production allows companies to sell clothes, phones and household item at affordable prices, so repairing an old item may not seem worthwhile, especially when a new product looks more modern and comes with a warranty. Another important factor is that repair services are often expensive, slow or hard to find. For example , fixing a broken phone screen or small kitchen appliance can cost almost as much as buying a new lost cost ones. In addition, many modern products are not designed to be repaired easily , so consumers may feel they have little choice. Therefore, people often choose to replace damaged items rather than repair them
