Cấu Trúc 4 Đoạn
Introduction
Paraphrase đề + trả lời ngắn 2 câu hỏi.
Formula:
In many countries, people increasingly replace broken items instead of repairing them. 
This trend is mainly caused by [reason 1] and [reason 2], and it may lead to [problem 1] and [problem 2].
Áp dụng:
In many countries, people increasingly replace broken items instead of repairing them. 
This trend is mainly caused by the low price of new products and the inconvenience of repair, and it may lead to more waste and a weaker repair culture.
Body 1: Reasons
Nói 2 lý do chính.
Formula:
One reason for this trend is that [reason 1]. 
This is because [explanation]. 
Another factor is [reason 2]. 
For example, [example]. 
As a result, people often find it easier to buy a replacement than to repair the old item.
Ý có thể dùng:
Reason 1: New products are cheaper than before.
Reason 2: Repair services are expensive, slow, or hard to find.
Reason 3: Advertising encourages people to buy newer models.
Reason 4: Modern products are sometimes not designed to be repaired easily.
Body 1 mẫu:
One reason for this trend is that many new products are now relatively cheap. Mass production allows companies to sell clothes, phones and household items at affordable prices, so repairing an old item may not seem worth the effort. Another factor is that repair services are often expensive or inconvenient. For example, fixing a broken phone screen can cost almost as much as buying a new low-cost device. As a result, many consumers choose replacement over repair.
Body 2: Problems/Effects
Nói 2 hậu quả chính.
Formula:
This habit can create several problems. 
The first problem is [problem 1]. 
This happens because [explanation]. 
A second issue is [problem 2]. 
If [condition], [negative result].
Ý có thể dùng:
Problem 1: More rubbish and electronic waste.
Problem 2: More pressure on natural resources.
Problem 3: People lose repair skills.
Problem 4: Consumerism becomes stronger.
Problem 5: Poorer people may feel pressure to buy new things.
Body 2 mẫu:
This habit can create several serious problems. The most obvious one is the growth of waste, especially electronic waste, which is difficult to recycle safely. When people throw away phones, appliances or clothes too quickly, more materials end up in landfills. A second problem is that society becomes more wasteful and dependent on constant consumption. If people always replace items instead of maintaining them, they may lose useful repair skills and place greater pressure on natural resources.
Conclusion
Tóm tắt reasons + problems. Không thêm ý mới.
Formula:
In conclusion, this trend is mainly the result of [reason 1] and [reason 2]. 
Its main consequences are [problem 1] and [problem 2].
Áp dụng:
In conclusion, people often throw broken things away because new products are cheap and repairs are inconvenient. This can increase waste, damage the environment and encourage a more disposable lifestyle.
Full Essay Formula
Introduction:
In many parts of the world, [paraphrase trend]. This is mainly because [reason 1] and [reason 2], and it may lead to [problem 1] and [problem 2].

Body 1:
One reason for this trend is [reason 1]. This is because [explanation]. Another reason is [reason 2]. For example, [example]. Therefore, [link back to why people buy new things].

Body 2:
This trend can cause several problems. The first is [problem 1], because [explanation]. Another serious issue is [problem 2]. If [condition], [negative result]. This shows why the throwaway habit can be harmful.

Conclusion:
In conclusion, [trend] is mainly caused by [reason 1] and [reason 2]. Its main effects are [problem 1] and [problem 2].
Useful Vocabulary
throwaway culture: văn hóa dùng rồi bỏ
repair broken items: sửa đồ hỏng
replace old products: thay thế sản phẩm cũ
mass production: sản xuất hàng loạt
electronic waste / e-waste: rác thải điện tử
landfills: bãi chôn lấp rác
natural resources: tài nguyên thiên nhiên
consumer habits: thói quen tiêu dùng
planned obsolescence: việc sản phẩm được thiết kế để nhanh lỗi thời
