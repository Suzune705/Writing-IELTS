As part of their education, students should spend a period of time living in a foreign country to learn its language and culture. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

## Opening

It is often argued that in order to explore languages and culture of a country , student should allocate a period of their time to live in that country. Personally , I firmly agree with this view on account of the fact that it is most effective way to fathom and comprehend totally a language as well as culture in a particular country 

## Body 1

The first reason why I support this view is that learning a new language is a daunting task. In fact , a lot of people who have been spending around 12 years of general education to master a language such as English can not rival those who learn it by travelling to its country. For example , for a child living in America , it  takes them several months in order to master its language rather than several years or even over a decade. Therefore , to become fluent in the local languages , it is necessary to student living in that foreign country. Beside that, living in foreign countries not only help student learn a language but also understand how to use it properly in daily life. This is because when they have environment to interact with local residents directly, they are more likely to learn how to use vocabulary they have studied to communicate. For this reason , travelling to a foreign country to learn a language bring clear benefit in terms of improving capacity of using and acquiring any languge.

## Body 2 

Another reasone is that exploring any local culture in a certain country requires  learners to immerse themselve in local environment , atmosphere and meet local residents. In present day, there many technologies such as television and social media platforms for exploring foreign cultures. Nonetheless, they only bring a certain level of efficiency and cannot help learner fathom them deeply. For example, in VietNam's culinary culture such as Banh Chung which is one of traditional dishes during Tet holiday , if a foreigner just research this kind of rice cake through several online sources , they can not savor its flavor as well as understand deeply about how it is made. For this reason , to explore and gain a deep understanding of a cultural heritage in any nation , student should spend a certaint amount of their time in that country.
