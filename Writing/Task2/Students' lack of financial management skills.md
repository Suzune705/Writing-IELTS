In many countries , students leave high school without understanding
how to manage their money. Why is this ?
What solutions can be suggested to help students
manage their finances effectively ?

It is widely observed that many school leavers lack basic financial literacy.
This essay will examine the reasons behind this issue and propose practical measureas to address it.

Thesis statement: The main reasons for student's lack of financial management skills are the absence of financial cirriculm in schools. This is because most educational systems prioritize academic subjects such as mathematics , science and literature over practical life skills like financial management. As a result, students lack the opportunity to learn about budgeting, saving , investing and other essential financial concepts before they take part in the labor market.


On the other hand, Many student's families may not often mention to their children about money management. This due to the fact that , money is often considered a taboo topic in many households, and parents may feel nervous when sharing about financial matters with their children. Moreover, they probably don't want their childrent to worry and think too much about money at a young age, because they want them to focus on their studies without any distractions. For example, to some students who are about to enter university, if they know much about situation of their family financial when it is shared by their parents, they might feel stressed and anxious whether their family can afford the tution fees or not.


To tackle this problem, it is essential to integrate financial education into the school curriculum and provide students with real-life financial experiences through workshops and internships that have expertises in financial management.From that, students can gain practical knowledge and skills that will help them make precise financial decisions in the future. Besides that, parents should also be more comfortable in discussing financial matters with their children. They can start by having open conversations about money, budgeting and saving from an early age. More importantly, for some parents, to avoid makeing their children feel stressed about financial issues, they just need to share the general concept of how to manage money effectively without going into specific details such as the family's financial situation and income. This way, students not only learn about some bacsic money management skills from their parents but also avoid feeling anxious about money issues.
